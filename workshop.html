UOW Expanding Minds Research Series

The Expanding Minds Research Series hosted by the School of Liberal Arts is a platform for academics based at and visiting the University of Wollongong to share their latest research and scholarship. All are most welcome and there is no need to register. All talks will be held in-person at the University of Wollongong’s Keiraville campus in the Research Hub, Building 19, Room 2072B.
